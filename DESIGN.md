# DESIGN — Funniest Lost Sock Status Update

## The entry

> I'm inside the piano now. Every wrong note is mine. The pianist blames the acoustics. I take the credit.

104 characters, 104 bytes, plain ASCII, one line, no trailing newline.

## Why this line

Built to win the judge's five criteria in order. (1) A status update in
the voice of a lost sock: first person, present tense, a sock reporting
from where it ended up — inside a piano. (2) A real laugh, not a smile:
the turn lands in the last four words, "I take the credit" — the sock
does not apologise for the wrong notes, it claims them. (3) A clear
comic premise the situation makes inevitable: a sock lodged in a piano
would muffle the strings, and a pianist would blame the room, not the
furniture — the joke is found in the piano, not assembled onto it.
(4) It sounds like a sock: small, domestic, reporting from inside
furniture, never a person wearing a sock as a costume — no human job,
no dating, no career. (5) It feels new: the piano lane is empty in the
534-title field, and the line sidesteps the flooded dryer/partner
material instead of repeating it.

## The empty lane

The field floods the dryer (50 titles), the lint union (24), the missing
partner (24), the dog (17), the fridge (15), the dust bunnies (12) and
the "Day N behind the dryer" diary format (90). The one-off lanes are
taken too: library book, wine-bottle cozy, scarecrow hat, trumpet,
suitcase to Bali, birdhouse, gnome hat. A piano is a place with its own
logic — hammers, strings, acoustics — that forces the punchline: the
wrong notes have to be somebody's fault, and the sock volunteers.

## The tournament

12 candidates in empty lanes, 66 pairwise games on the exact five
criteria, then the winner against the resolved one-liner winners and
the strongest field lines: 11-0 internally, 10-0 against the
references. Full table: CANDIDATES.md. Runner-up: "I work the
lost-property desk now…" — lost only on criterion 4, because a clerk
is a human job. The library-book sock ties this line on criteria 1–4
and loses on 5: the piano lane is empty, and this sock claims credit
where the bookmark only endures.

## The program

`main.py`: one constant, gate guards mirroring the competition gate
(1-120 chars, code points and bytes, not only whitespace, one line),
no trailing newline. No judge-addressed text, no input, no clock, no
randomness, no network.
